Meet Your Coach

Jackson Anderson

Jackson Anderson is the owner and founder of Fitness & Strength LLC and a certified personal trainer who has helped clients build stronger bodies, healthier habits, and lasting confidence since 2020.

As an athlete, Jackson brings firsthand experience in physique-focused and endurance training. He competed in the USBF Wisconsin Warrior competition in summer 2026 and has completed a multitude of triathlons across the US.

His coaching philosophy is simple: meet people where they are, build a plan that fits their life, and help them grow into their full potential through consistent, purposeful action.
